Operating Systems Concepts

Mutual Exclusion

Forks act as shared resources. Only one philosopher can hold a fork at any given time, implemented using threading.Lock (Mutex).

Deadlock

Occurs when all 5 philosophers acquire their left fork and wait indefinitely for the right fork, creating a circular wait condition.

Prevention

Using a Semaphore with MAX_ALLOWED = 4. Limiting the number of concurrent philosophers attempting to eat breaks the circular wait condition.
